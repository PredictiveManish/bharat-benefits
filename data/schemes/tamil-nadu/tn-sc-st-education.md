# Tamil Nadu Free Education Scheme for SC/ST Students

> **Demo Caveat:** This file is a curated summary for demonstration purposes only. Scholarship
> amounts and eligibility criteria may change. Always verify on the official portal:
> https://scholarships.tn.gov.in

## Purpose
Tamil Nadu government provides comprehensive educational support to SC/ST students from pre-primary
to post-graduate level, including scholarships, free education, and hostel facilities.

## Who It Helps
- SC/ST students in Tamil Nadu
- Students from economically weaker sections
- Students in government and government-aided schools/colleges
- Orphan students and students with disabilities
- Students pursuing professional courses

## Eligibility Summary
- Must be SC/ST resident of Tamil Nadu
- Must be studying in government or government-aided institution
- Family income limits vary by scheme (typically ₹2 lakh/year for most)
- Minimum academic performance required (varies by scheme)
- Must not be availing similar benefits from central government simultaneously

## Benefits
- **Free education** from pre-primary to post-graduate level
- **Scholarships**: ₹500-₹3,000 per month depending on education level
- **Free textbooks and study materials**
- **Hostel facilities** with subsidized accommodation
- **Stipend for professional courses**: Up to ₹3,000/month
- **Laptop/Tab distribution** for meritorious students
- **Travel allowance** for students from remote areas
- **Special coaching** for competitive exams

## Common Documents Required
- Aadhaar card
- Caste certificate (SC/ST) from competent authority
- Income certificate
- School/college admission proof
- Marksheet of previous year
- Bank account details linked to Aadhaar
- Domicile certificate of Tamil Nadu

## How to Apply / Next Steps
1. Visit https://scholarships.tn.gov.in for online application
2. Or apply through school/college administration
3. Submit documents for verification
4. Track application status online
5. Helpline: **044-25300500** (Tamil Nadu Scholarship Helpline)
