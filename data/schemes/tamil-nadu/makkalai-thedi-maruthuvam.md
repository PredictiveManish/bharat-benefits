# Tamil Nadu Chief Minister's Comprehensive Health Insurance Scheme (Makkalai Thedi Maruthuvam)

> **Demo Caveat:** This file is a curated summary for demonstration purposes only. Benefit amounts
> and eligibility criteria may change. Always verify on the official portal:
> https://tnhealth.tn.gov.in

## Purpose
Tamil Nadu's flagship health insurance scheme providing worldwide cashless treatment coverage
to identified families. The scheme offers higher coverage than PM-JAY and includes worldwide
coverage for certain treatments.

## Who It Helps
- Families identified through TN Aarodhu Aarivu scheme survey
- BPL families in Tamil Nadu
- Asoliar card holders
- Daily wage workers and informal sector workers
- All identified families regardless of caste or religion

## Eligibility Summary
- Must be identified through TN Aarodhu Aarivu survey
- Must hold valid Asoliar card
- Tamil Nadu domicile requirement
- One card per family
- No income ceiling for identified families
- Includes families identified in 2022 survey

## Benefits
- **Coverage up to ₹5 lakh per family** per year (same as PM-JAY)
- **Worldwide coverage** for specific treatments (cardiac, oncology, neurological)
- **No restriction** on number of families per hospital
- **Pre-existing conditions** covered from day one
- **Free medicines and diagnostics** at hospitals
- **Ambulance service** free of cost
- **No age limit** for beneficiaries
- **Women priority** in certain treatment categories

## Common Documents Required
- Asoliar card
- Aadhaar card
- Ration card
- BPL certificate (if applicable)
- Photo identity proof
- Bank account details (for any monetary benefits)

## How to Apply / Next Steps
1. Check eligibility at https://tnhealth.tn.gov.in
2. Asoliar cards are issued based on survey identification
3. Visit empanelled hospitals for cashless treatment
4. Check hospital list on TN health portal
5. Helpline: **104** (Tamil Nadu Health Helpline) or **1800-425-3668** (Amma Health Helpline)
