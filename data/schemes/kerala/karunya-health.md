# Kerala Karunya Health Insurance Scheme

> **Demo Caveat:** This file is a curated summary for demonstration purposes only. Benefit amounts
> and eligibility criteria may change. Always verify on the official portal:
> https://keralamhealthmission.gov.in

## Purpose
Karunya is Kerala's state health insurance scheme providing comprehensive health coverage to
families identified through the Karunya card system. It works alongside PM-JAY to provide
additional coverage for Kerala residents.

## Who It Helps
- Karunya card holders (identified through SECC and state-level surveys)
- BPL families in Kerala
- Daily wage workers and informal sector workers
- Fisherfolk communities
- Tribal communities in Kerala
- Weaker sections and marginalised communities

## Eligibility Summary
- Must hold a valid Karunya card issued by Kerala government
- Family must be identified as BPL through state survey
- Kerala domicile requirement
- One card per family
- Cards renewed periodically based on updated surveys

## Benefits
- **Cashless treatment** at empanelled hospitals in Kerala
- **Coverage for** surgery, medical treatment, ICU, medicines
- **Pre-existing diseases** covered
- **No age restriction**
- **Family floater** basis - entire family covered
- **Ambulance services** included
- **Special coverage** for maternal and child health

## Common Documents Required
- Karunya card
- Aadhaar card
- BPL certificate
- Ration card
- Photo identity proof
- Bank account details

## How to Apply / Next Steps
1. Karunya cards are issued based on government surveys
2. Check status at https://keralamhealthmission.gov.in
3. Visit empanelled hospitals for treatment
4. Helpline: **104** (Kerala Health Helpline)
