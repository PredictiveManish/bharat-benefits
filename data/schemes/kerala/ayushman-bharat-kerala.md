# Ayushman Bharat – Pradhan Mantri Jan Arogya Yojana (PM-JAY) Kerala Edition

> **Demo Caveat:** This file is a curated summary for demonstration purposes only. Benefit amounts,
> eligibility rules, and hospital lists change periodically. Always verify current details on the
> official portal: https://keralamhealthmission.gov.in

## Purpose
Kerala's state-enhanced health insurance program built on PM-JAY framework, providing additional
benefits and wider coverage for Kerala residents. The Kerala Health Mission operates PM-JAY
with state-specific modifications for better healthcare access.

## Who It Helps
- Families listed in SECC 2011 data for Kerala
- BPL (Below Poverty Line) card holders in Kerala
- Aswathy card holders (Kerala's health identification card)
- All residents of Kerala with priority to vulnerable sections
- APL (Above Poverty Line) families in certain special categories

## Eligibility Summary
- Kerala residents with valid Aswathy card or BPL card
- SECC 2011 listed families in Kerala
- Families with members having chronic illnesses (cancer, kidney disease, heart conditions)
- Pregnant women and children under 18 years
- Senior citizens above 60 years
- Persons with disabilities

## Benefits
- **Enhanced coverage**: Up to ₹5 lakh per family per year under PM-JAY
- **Additional state benefits**: Kerala provides supplementary coverage for certain treatments
- **Free treatment** at empanelled government and private hospitals
- **Cashless and paperless** access to healthcare services
- **Pre-existing conditions** covered from day one
- **Medicines and diagnostics** included in package rates
- **Transportation assistance** for rural patients to reach hospitals

## Common Documents Required
- Aswathy card (Kerala health identification card)
- Aadhaar card
- BPL card (if applicable)
- Ration card
- Ayushman card / e-card
- Photo identity proof

## How to Apply / Next Steps
1. Visit https://keralamhealthmission.gov.in to check eligibility
2. Apply for Aswathy card at nearest Health Centre or online
3. Visit empanelled hospital's Ayushman Mitra desk
4. Generate Ayushman card for cashless treatment
5. Helpline: **104** (Kerala Health Helpline) or **14555** (PM-JAY National Helpline)
