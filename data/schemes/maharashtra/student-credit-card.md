# Maharashtra Chief Minister's Student Credit Card Scheme

> **Demo Caveat:** This file is a curated summary for demonstration purposes only. Benefit amounts
> and eligibility criteria may change. Always verify on the official portal:
> https://scholarship.maharashtra.gov.in

## Purpose
Maharashtra government provides education loans with interest subsidies to students from
economically weaker sections to pursue higher education and professional courses.

## Who It Helps
- Students from Maharashtra belonging to EWS, OBC, and minority communities
- Students pursuing professional courses (engineering, medical, management, etc.)
- Students from low-income families
- First-generation learners

## Eligibility Summary
- Must be a permanent resident of Maharashtra
- Family income below ₹8 lakh per year for general category
- Family income below ₹5 lakh per year for SC/ST/OBC
- Must have admission in recognized institution
- Minimum marks criteria varies by course

## Benefits
- **Education loan** up to ₹10 lakh for professional courses
- **Interest subsidy** of 3% on education loans
- **Collateral-free loans** up to ₹7.5 lakh
- **Tuition fee coverage** including coaching fees
- **Study material allowance**
- **Hostel fee support**

## Common Documents Required
- Aadhaar card
- Domicile certificate of Maharashtra
- Income certificate
- Caste certificate (if applicable)
- Admission proof from institution
- Marksheet of qualifying exam
- Bank account details

## How to Apply / Next Steps
1. Visit https://scholarship.maharashtra.gov.in
2. Register and fill application form
3. Upload required documents
4. Submit for verification
5. Helpline: **022-22020009** (Maharashtra Scholarship Helpline)
