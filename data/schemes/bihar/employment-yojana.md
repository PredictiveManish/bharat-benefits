# Bihar Chief Minister's Employment Yojana

> **Demo Caveat:** This file is a curated summary for demonstration purposes only. Benefit amounts
> and eligibility criteria may change. Always verify on the official portal:
> https://employment.bihar.gov.in

## Purpose
Bihar government initiative to provide employment opportunities and skill development to youth
of Bihar, focusing on government job placements and private sector partnerships.

## Who It Helps
- Youth of Bihar aged 18-35 years
- Graduates and post-graduates from Bihar
- ITI and diploma holders from Bihar
- Daily wage seekers in rural and urban Bihar
- Women seeking employment opportunities

## Eligibility Summary
- Must be permanent resident of Bihar
- Age between 18-35 years
- Minimum educational qualification as specified per vacancy
- Must have passed Bihar board examinations or recognized university
- No criminal record

## Benefits
- **Government job placements** in various departments
- **Skill training** in government-approved centres
- **Stipend during training** period
- **Job placement assistance** in private sector
- **Self-employment guidance** and micro-finance linkage
- **Transport allowance** for job seekers

## Common Documents Required
- Aadhaar card
- Educational certificates
- Caste certificate (if applicable)
- Domicile certificate of Bihar
- Character certificate
- Photographs
- Bank account details

## How to Apply / Next Steps
1. Register at https://employment.bihar.gov.in
2. Fill application form for desired vacancies
3. Upload documents and submit
4. Prepare for written test/interview
5. Helpline: **1800-345-0033** (Bihar Employment Helpline)
