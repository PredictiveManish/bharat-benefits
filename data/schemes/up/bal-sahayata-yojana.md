# Uttar Pradesh Mukhyamantri Bal Sahayata Yojana

> https://up.gov.in

## Purpose
UP government provides financial assistance to families in case of death or permanent disability
of the breadwinner due to accident or natural causes, to support the family's education and
livelihood.

## Who It Helps
- Families of daily wage workers in Uttar Pradesh
- Families where breadwinner is engaged in manual labour
- BPL families in UP
- Families affected by accidents or natural calamities
- Widows and orphaned children

## Eligibility Summary
- Must be resident of Uttar Pradesh
- Family must have breadwinner engaged in manual/daily wage work
- Family income below specified threshold
- Must have registered with local administration
- Accident or death must be reported to authorities

## Benefits
- **Financial assistance** of ₹2-5 lakh in case of death of breadwinner
- **Monthly pension** for widows in certain cases
- **Education support** for children of deceased breadwinner
- **One-time relief** for families affected by natural disasters
- **Medical expense coverage** for accident cases

## Common Documents Required
- Aadhaar card
- Death certificate or disability certificate
- Ration card
- BPL card
- Income certificate
- Bank account details
- Police report (in case of accident)

## How to Apply / Next Steps
1. Report incident to local police and administration
2. Apply at Block Development Office or Tehsil office
3. Submit supporting documents
4. Verification by local authorities
5. Helpline: **1077** (UP Emergency Helpline)
