# Pradhan Mantri Suraksha Bima Yojana (PMSBY)

> **Demo Caveat:** This file is a curated summary for demonstration purposes only. Premium rates
> and coverage details may change. Always verify on the official portal:
> https://pmsby.gov.in

## Purpose
PMSBY is an accident insurance scheme providing coverage for death and disability due to
accident. It is an auto-debit scheme linked to bank accounts.

## Who It Helps
- Individuals with a bank account aged 18-70 years
- All bank account holders eligible for auto-debit
- Low-income families seeking affordable insurance coverage

## Eligibility Summary
- Age between 18-70 years
- Must have a bank account
- Must give consent for auto-debit of premium
- One policy per individual
- Renewal is annual

## Benefits
- **Death cover**: ₹2 lakh in case of death due to accident
- **Full disability cover**: ₹2 lakh
- **Partial disability cover**: ₹1 lakh
- **Low premium**: ₹12 per year (auto-debited from bank account)
- **Coverage period**: June 1 to June 30 each year

## Common Documents Required
- Aadhaar card
- Bank account details
- Consent for auto-debit
- KYC documents

## How to Apply / Next Steps
1. Visit your bank branch with Aadhaar and bank account
2. Fill the PMSBY enrollment form
3. Provide consent for annual premium auto-debit
4. Premium of ₹12 will be auto-debited from your account
5. Helpline: Check with your bank branch
