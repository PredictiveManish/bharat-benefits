# Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana (PMAY) – Urban

> **Demo Caveat:** This file is a curated summary for demonstration purposes only. Benefit amounts,
> eligibility criteria, and implementation guidelines may change. Always verify on the official portal:
> https://pmay-urban.gov.in

## Purpose
Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana (Urban) aims to provide affordable housing to the urban poor by 2022.
The scheme provides central assistance to states/UTs for construction of houses for the urban
poor belonging to EIG, EWS and LIG categories.

## Who It Helps
- Economically Weaker Sections (EWS): Annual income up to ₹3 lakh
- Low Income Group (LIG): Annual income ₹3-₹12 lakh
- Economically Impaired Section (EIG): Annual income ₹12-₹18 lakh
- Urban homeless families and slum dwellers of cities/towns

## Eligibility Summary
- Must not have an pucca house or kutcha house anywhere in the name of family members.
- Must not have availed any other housing benefit from the Central or State Government.
- Priority to SC/ST, widows, physically challenged, transgender, and minority communities.
- No upper age limit.
- Women must be included as primary homeowner or joint owner.

## Benefits
- **Central Assistance**: Up to ₹1.5 lakh per house in hilly states and North-East.
- **Credit Linked Subsidy Scheme (CLSS)**: Interest subsidy on home loans:
  - EWS: 6.5% subsidy on loan up to ₹6 lakh (subsidy amount ₹2.67 lakh)
  - LIG: 4% subsidy on loan up to ₹9 lakh (subsidy amount ₹2.52 lakh)
  - EIG: 3% subsidy on loan up to ₹12 lakh (subsidy amount ₹2.04 lakh)
- **Affordable Housing in Existing Cities**: Financial assistance for development of affordable housing.
- **Borrower's Equity**: 1% of eligible project cost for developers.

## Common Documents Required
- Aadhaar card
- Income proof (salary slip, IT return, or self-declaration)
- Bank account details
- Caste certificate (for SC/ST applicants)
- Ration card
- Photo of current residence

## How to Apply / Next Steps
1. Visit https://pmay-urban.gov.in and check eligibility.
2. Apply through the PMAY-U portal or visit the nearest district-level PMAY office.
3. For CLSS, approach banks that are PMAY-U partners.
4. Check beneficiary list on the state PMAY portal.
5. Helpline: **1900** (toll-free) or **011-23388888**
