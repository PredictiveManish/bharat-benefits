# Pradhan Mantri Jan Dhan Yojana (PMJDY)

> **Demo Caveat:** This file is a curated summary for demonstration purposes only. Scheme guidelines
> may be updated. Always verify on the official portal: https://pmjdy.gov.in

## Purpose
Pradhan Mantri Jan Dhan Yojana is a financial inclusion mission aimed at ensuring access to
financial services (banking, savings, credit, insurance, pension) for unbanked households across India.

## Who It Helps
- Unbanked households and individuals
- Low-income families
- Daily wage workers
- Women who lack access to formal banking
- Migrant workers
- Senior citizens without bank accounts

## Eligibility Summary
- Any Indian citizen above 10 years of age can open a regular account.
- Children between 10-18 years can open a 'Bal' account with certain restrictions.
- No minimum balance required.
- No minimum initial deposit required (₹100 for basic savings, ₹0 for basic no-frills).
- One account per household (original goal).

## Benefits
- **Zero balance account**: No minimum balance requirement.
- **Accidental insurance cover**: ₹2 lakh accidental death cover for account holders
  (opened after August 28, 2018).
- **Life insurance cover**: ₹30,000 life insurance cover for eligible account holders.
- **Overdraft facility**: Up to ₹5,000 after 6 months of satisfactory operation.
- **RuPay debit card**: With UPI, ATM, and point-of-sale capabilities.
- **Direct Benefit Transfer**: DBT of government subsidies directly into accounts.
- **Pension access**: Access to Atal Pension Yojana and other pension products.
- **Mobile banking**: Basic banking services via mobile apps.

## Common Documents Required
- Aadhaar card (mandatory for KYC)
- Passport-size photograph
- Proof of address (if different from Aadhaar)
- Proof of identity (if different from Aadhaar)
- Passport, voter ID, or driving license (as additional ID)

## How to Apply / Next Steps
1. Visit any bank that is a PMJDY partner (all public sector banks, many private banks, RRBs, and small finance banks).
2. Fill the account opening form available at the bank branch.
3. Submit Aadhaar card and photograph.
4. Receive RuPay debit card within 30 days.
5. Activate UPI and mobile banking through the bank.
6. Helpline: **1800-711-0004** (PMJDY Helpline)
